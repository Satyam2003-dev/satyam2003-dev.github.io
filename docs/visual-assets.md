# Decorative visual assets

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then compressed to WebP for local hosting. No external image requests are needed at runtime. These are illustrations, not project screenshots or a portrait of Satyam.

| Asset | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `public/assets/images/security-hero.webp` | Wide mountain hero; anonymous figure on a ridge, dark space for live text |
| `public/assets/images/learning-journal.webp` | Learning journal cover; workstation and cybersecurity hologram |
| `public/assets/images/secure-delivery.webp` | Technical article cover; glass infinity loop and shield |

Prompt directions used:

1. Cinematic panoramic mountain landscape at blue twilight, anonymous lone explorer on a ridge toward the right, layered mountains and clouds, subtle warm light on the horizon. Dark spacious left side for webpage typography. Premium realistic portfolio art, navy and icy cyan, no text, logos, or watermark.
2. Premium cybersecurity editorial cover: translucent blue and cyan infinity loop with a central shield/check, polished glass and metal, subtle network lines against a deep navy background. Crisp luminous highlights, restrained composition, no words, logos, or watermark.
3. Premium editorial cybersecurity learning cover: a dark workstation with a laptop and a cyan holographic shield/network above the desk, soft blue lighting and depth, cinematic realistic materials. Navy and cyan palette, no readable text, logos, or watermark.

The page renders headings, categories, and buttons as accessible HTML rather than text baked into images. Image dimensions reserve space; the hero loads eagerly and covers load lazily.
